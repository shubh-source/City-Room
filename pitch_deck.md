## 💰 5. Business Model

How we generate revenue:

- **Freemium Subscriptions:** Free basic features, with premium tiers for advanced owner tools (Cityroom) and exclusive couple features (Vlynxly).
- **Featured Listings (Cityroom):** Small fee for room owners to boost their listings to the top of search results.
- **In-App Purchases (Vlynxly):** Curated date packages and premium customization options.
- **Targeted Ads/Partnerships:** Local businesses, cafes, and event organizers.

## 🥊 6. Competition

**Cityroom vs. OYO / NoBroker:**
- *Competitors* focus heavily on Tier-1 cities, hotels, or whole apartments with high brokerage/fees.
- *Cityroom* targets the untapped Tier 2/3 room-rental market with a hyper-local, zero-friction approach.

**Vlynxly vs. Tinder / Bumble / WhatsApp:**
- *Competitors* are built for singles (dating) or general messaging.
- *Vlynxly* is exclusively for established couples focusing on relationship enrichment and shared experiences.
